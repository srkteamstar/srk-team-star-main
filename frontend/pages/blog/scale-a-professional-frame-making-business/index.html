Business Growth

Starting and scaling a professional frame-making business

Growth in frame making comes from turning individual skill into a reliable process that another trained person can repeat.

24 July 2026 · 3 min read

Define a clear starting offer

Choose the customers and frame types you can serve consistently with the available space, equipment and working capital. A focused range makes purchasing, quoting, training and quality control easier during the early stage.

Document what every price includes and how long each type of job normally takes. Clear promises protect margins and give customers a reason to return.

Standardise before adding speed

Create standard methods for measuring, cutting, joining, inspection and packing. Store tools and materials in fixed locations. Use job records that prevent dimensions and requirements from being rewritten at every station.

When the method is stable, training becomes easier and defects become traceable. Speed added to an unstable process usually increases rework.

Find the actual constraint

Track orders completed, rework, material loss and time waiting between stages. The visible machine is not always the bottleneck; approvals, material selection, glass preparation or packing can limit the whole business.

Invest where additional capacity changes the number of correct jobs shipped. Sometimes that means machinery. At other times it means better storage, an additional workbench or clearer scheduling.

Protect cash and service as volume grows

Plan material purchases around confirmed demand and maintain realistic delivery commitments. Keep critical consumables and wear parts available without allowing slow-moving stock to absorb all working capital.

Customers remember communication as clearly as the finished frame. A growing workshop should preserve the direct accountability that built its reputation in the first place.

Workshop note

Use these principles as a starting point and follow the operating, safety and maintenance instructions supplied with your specific equipment.
