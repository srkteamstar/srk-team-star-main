From a local photo studio to a global manufacturing partner.

SRK Team Star grew from the experience of Malik Studio and a determination to keep learning, building and solving real problems for the picture framing industry.

  1. Established 1985

    Malik Studio begins

    The journey started in Gohana with passport photographs, school portraits and family pictures. A hands-on understanding of photography, presentation and customer service became the foundation for everything that followed.

  2. The turning point

    Looking beyond familiar work

    Malik Studio was doing well, but the work began to feel limited in its room for learning and long-term growth. That realisation encouraged us to look closely at the wider industry around photographs and frames.

  3. A gap in the market

    SRK Team Star takes shape

    We saw an opportunity in manufacturing machines for the photo frame industry. Building on our studio background and first-hand knowledge of frame makers' needs, we started SRK Team Star to create practical, dependable production solutions.

  4. Today and beyond

    Made in Gohana. Serving the world.

    Today, our machinery and framing solutions support customers across India and beyond, including Bhutan, Nepal and growing markets in Africa. We continue to learn from every installation and build for the people who rely on our machines each day.
